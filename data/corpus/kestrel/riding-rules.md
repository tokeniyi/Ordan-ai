# Riding rules

These rules keep you safe and keep your theft and accident cover valid.

## Who can ride

Riders must be at least 16 years old. Members must be 18 or over to sign the agreement, so a parent or guardian signs for riders aged 16 or 17. There is no upper age limit.

## Helmets and lights

Port Aven law does not require helmets on electric bikes that stop helping above 25 km/h, but we strongly recommend one. Every membership can add a helmet for €3 a month. The lights switch on automatically when it gets dark and must not be covered.

## Loads and passengers

The K1 city bike is for one rider only. The rider and their luggage together may weigh up to 120 kg, and the rear rack takes up to 25 kg. The K2 cargo bike can carry up to 200 kg in total, including the rider, and up to two children aged six or under in the box with their seat belts fastened. Child seats on the rack are not allowed on either bike.

## Where you can ride

Ride on roads and cycle lanes. Riding on pavements is not allowed in Port Aven, except where a sign shows a shared path. Kestrel bikes must not be used off road, in races, or on beaches, because sand and salt water damage the motor.

## Changes to the bike

Do not change or tune the motor, the speed limit or the battery. Simple accessories that clip on, such as a phone holder, a basket or panniers, are fine. Anything that needs tools, such as a different saddle, must be fitted by our mechanics.

## Taking the bike abroad

You can take the bike on holiday in any country of the European Union for up to 30 days in a row. Tell us in the app before you go so your theft cover stays valid. Battery swaps and roadside help are only available in our area.
