# The app and your account

The Kestrel app runs on iPhone and Android. You use it to manage your membership, book services, swap batteries and get help.

## Signing in

Sign in with your email address. We send a six-digit code instead of using a password. The code expires after ten minutes. You can turn on two-step sign-in under Account, then Security, which also asks for a code from an authenticator app.

## Unlocking the bike without the key

If you forget your key, you can unlock the frame lock from the app. Stand within two metres of the bike, open the bike screen and hold the unlock button for three seconds. This needs Bluetooth to be switched on. The chain lock still needs the key.

## Payment details

Change your card under Account, then Payment. The new card is used from the next payment. Invoices for every payment are under Account, then Invoices, and can be downloaded as PDF files, which is useful if your employer pays for your bike.

## Sharing the bike

Only the member may ride a Commuter, Weekender or Annual Commuter bike. On the Family plan you can add one more adult rider from the same address under Account, then Riders. The second rider gets their own sign-in but cannot change the membership.

## Your data

The app records rides only to show you your distance and to find the bike if it is stolen. Ride history is kept for 12 months and then deleted. You can download all your data or ask for it to be deleted under Account, then Privacy. Deleting your data is only possible after your membership has ended and the bike has been returned.

## Notifications

The app sends reminders about services, payments and low battery. You can turn each kind of notification off under Settings, except messages about safety recalls, which are always sent.
