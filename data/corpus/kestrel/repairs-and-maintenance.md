# Repairs and maintenance

Servicing is included in every membership. You never pay for wear and tear, such as brake pads, tyres, chains or bulbs.

## Regular service

Every bike gets a free full service every six months. We send a reminder in the app two weeks before it is due. A mechanic can service the bike at your home in a two-hour slot, or you can drop it at the depot on Harbour Street and pick it up the same day. A service checks the brakes, gears, tyres, lights, battery health and frame, and takes about 45 minutes.

## Booking a repair

If something stops working between services, open the app and choose Report a problem. Add a short description and a photo if you can. Most repairs are booked within two working days. If the bike cannot be ridden safely, we lend you a replacement bike while yours is fixed.

## Flat tyres and roadside help

Our roadside team fixes flat tyres and other breakdowns anywhere inside the ring road. Call the help line from the app and a mechanic will reach you within 90 minutes between 7:00 and 21:00. Outside the ring road, or outside those hours, we collect the bike the next day and you can travel home at our cost with a taxi refund of up to €30.

## What you pay for

Damage caused by misuse or an accident that was your fault is charged at cost, up to a limit of €150 for each incident. Common charges are listed below.

| Item | Charge |
| --- | --- |
| Lost bike key | €25 |
| Lost charger | €45 |
| Broken mirror or bell | €10 |
| Bent front wheel | €60 |

We always send a photo and a quote before charging you, and you can dispute a charge within 14 days.

## Looking after your bike

Keep the bike indoors or under cover when you can. Wipe the chain after riding in the rain and check the tyre pressure once a month: the right pressure is printed on the side of each tyre. Do not use a pressure washer, because water can get into the motor and the battery contacts.
