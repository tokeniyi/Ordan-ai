# Delivery and returns

We bring your bike to your door, set it up, and show you how everything works.

## Where we deliver

We deliver anywhere in Port Aven and the towns of Millbrook and Saltmarsh. Delivery inside Port Aven is free. Delivery to Millbrook or Saltmarsh costs €15, charged once with your first payment. We do not deliver to the islands yet.

## Delivery times

Choose a delivery slot when you sign up. The earliest slot is usually two working days later. Slots are two hours long, between 8:00 and 20:00 on weekdays and between 9:00 and 17:00 on Saturdays. There are no deliveries on Sundays or public holidays. You can change your slot in the app until 18:00 the day before.

## At the handover

The rider must be at the handover to sign for the bike and show photo ID. The person delivering adjusts the saddle and handlebars to your height, pairs the bike with your app and takes you for a short test ride. Allow about 20 minutes. If nobody is home, we wait 15 minutes and then rebook, and a missed delivery costs €20.

## Returning the bike

When your membership ends, we collect the bike from the address on your account. Choose a collection slot in the app at least three days ahead. Please charge the battery and have the key, charger and lock ready. You can also return the bike yourself to the depot on Harbour Street during opening hours, Monday to Saturday from 8:00 to 18:00.

## Moving house

If you move within our delivery area, update your address in the app. The bike stays with you, and nothing changes in your membership. If you move outside the area, we collect the bike and end your membership without the usual notice period.
