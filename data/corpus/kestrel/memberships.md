# Memberships and prices

Kestrel Cycle Club rents electric bikes by subscription in Port Aven. Every membership includes the bike, a lock, a charger, servicing and theft cover. You choose a plan when you sign up and can change it at any time.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Bike | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Commuter | €29 a month | K1 city bike | Riding most days |
| Weekender | €15 a month | K1 city bike, Friday to Monday only | Weekend trips |
| Family | €49 a month | K2 cargo bike | Carrying children or shopping |
| Annual Commuter | €290 a year | K1 city bike | Saving two months' fees |

Students with a valid card from a Port Aven college get 20% off the Commuter and Annual Commuter plans. The discount is checked once a year.

## Deposit

There is no deposit for the Commuter, Weekender and Annual Commuter plans. The Family plan has a refundable deposit of €100, because the cargo bike costs more to replace. The deposit is returned within 10 working days after the bike comes back undamaged.

## Changing your plan

You can move to another plan from the app under Account, then Membership. An upgrade starts straight away and you pay the difference for the rest of the month. A downgrade starts on your next billing date. Moving to or from the Family plan means swapping the bike, which we arrange with a delivery slot.

## Pausing your membership

If you are away, you can pause your membership for up to two months in each calendar year. You keep the bike at home or return it to the depot, and you pay nothing while it is paused. A pause must be at least seven days long. Pauses are not available on the Annual Commuter plan, because it is already discounted.

## Cancelling

Monthly plans can be cancelled with 14 days' notice from the app. We collect the bike on or after the last day, and your final payment covers the notice period. Annual Commuter memberships can be cancelled in the first 30 days for a full refund. After that, the remaining months are refunded minus a €40 early-cancellation fee.

## Billing

Payments are taken on the same day each month, starting on the day the bike is delivered. We accept debit and credit cards. If a payment fails, we try again after three days. After two failed attempts the bike is locked remotely until the balance is paid.
