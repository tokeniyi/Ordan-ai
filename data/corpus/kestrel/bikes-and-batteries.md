# Bikes and batteries

Kestrel has two bikes. Both have a motor that helps you pedal up to 25 km/h, hydraulic disc brakes, built-in lights and a frame lock.

## The K1 city bike

The K1 is a light step-through bike for commuting. It weighs 22 kg, has seven gears and fits riders from 155 cm to 195 cm tall. The battery gives a range of about 70 km in the standard assist mode and 45 km in boost mode. The rear rack carries up to 25 kg.

## The K2 cargo bike

The K2 has a long front box with a bench and seat belts for two children up to six years old, or room for about four shopping bags. It weighs 38 kg and has a stronger motor for hills. Its range is about 55 km with an empty box and 40 km fully loaded. A rain cover for the box is included with the Family plan.

## Charging

Each bike comes with a charger that plugs into any normal wall socket. The battery unlocks with the same key as the bike and can be taken inside to charge. A full charge from empty takes about four and a half hours. Charging to 80% takes about three hours, which is enough for most days.

The battery lasts longest if you keep it between 20% and 80% and charge it at room temperature. Do not charge it below 0 °C or leave it in direct sun on a hot day.

## Battery swap stations

If you run low while you are out, you can swap your battery for a full one at any of our six swap stations, including the central depot on Harbour Street and the station at Port Aven Central. Scan the QR code on the locker with the app, put your battery in, and take the charged one that unlocks. Swaps are free and unlimited for every plan.

## Riding in cold weather

Lithium batteries hold less charge in the cold. Below 5 °C, expect about a quarter less range than usual. Keeping the battery indoors overnight and fitting it just before you ride helps. The display shows the remaining range based on how you have been riding, so check it before a long trip.

## Battery problems

If the battery will not charge, try another socket first and check that the charger light turns green when the battery is full. If it still does not charge, report it in the app. We replace faulty batteries free of charge, usually within two working days.
