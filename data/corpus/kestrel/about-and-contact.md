# About Kestrel and how to contact us

Kestrel Cycle Club was founded in 2019 by two bike mechanics, Ines Duarte and Tomas Wren, who wanted to make electric bikes affordable without the cost of buying one. We started with 40 bikes from a workshop on Harbour Street. Today we look after about 3,200 bikes across Port Aven, Millbrook and Saltmarsh, and every one is serviced in our own workshop.

## Getting help

The quickest way to get help is the chat in the app, which is answered by our support team from 7:00 to 22:00 every day. We aim to reply within 15 minutes. You can also call us on +353 21 555 0142 from 8:00 to 20:00 on weekdays and from 9:00 to 17:00 at weekends. Emails to help@kestrel.example are answered within one working day.

For breakdowns, always use the help line in the app, because it shows us where you are.

## The depot

Our depot and workshop is at 14 Harbour Street, Port Aven. It is open Monday to Saturday from 8:00 to 18:00 and closed on Sundays. You can drop off a bike for service, return a bike, collect a spare battery or try the K2 cargo bike before switching plans.

## Complaints

If you are not happy with how we handled something, reply to any support message with the word Complaint. A team lead will reply within three working days. If we cannot agree, you can take the complaint to the Port Aven Consumer Board, which is free.

## Sustainability

Old batteries are sent back to the manufacturer for recycling, and worn parts are recycled through a local scrap partner. Since 2023, our delivery vans have been replaced by K2 cargo bikes with trailers for every delivery inside the ring road.
