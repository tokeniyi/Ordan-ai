# Theft and accidents

Every membership includes cover for theft. Accidents are rare, but it helps to know what to do before one happens.

## If your bike is stolen

Report the theft to the police and get a crime reference number. Then report it in the app within 24 hours of noticing it missing, and add the reference number. If the bike was locked with the frame lock and the supplied chain lock to a fixed object, you pay a theft excess of €80 and we deliver a replacement bike within three working days.

If the bike was not locked with both locks, or you report the theft more than 24 hours late, the excess is €400. Every bike has a GPS tracker, so we can sometimes recover it. If we find it undamaged within seven days, the excess is refunded.

## If you have an accident

First make sure you and everyone else are safe, and call 112 if anyone is hurt. Take photos of the scene and the bike, and swap names and contact details with anyone else involved. Do not ride the bike again until it has been checked, even if it looks fine: a crash can damage the frame or the brakes in ways you cannot see.

Report the accident in the app within 48 hours. We collect the bike, check it and lend you a replacement in the meantime.

## Who pays

Kestrel's insurance covers damage to the bike whatever caused the accident, minus the damage charges listed on the repairs page when the accident was your fault. It also covers your legal liability up to €1,000,000 if you injure someone or damage their property while riding a Kestrel bike. It does not cover your own injuries, so we recommend personal accident insurance.

## Lost keys and locks

A lost key costs €25 and we post you a new one within two working days. If you lose the chain lock, the replacement costs €35. Until the new lock arrives, keep the bike indoors, because the theft cover needs both locks.
